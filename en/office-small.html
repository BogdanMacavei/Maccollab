Location & Access

You will find us at Strada Horia 22A, Brașov, in an easily accessible location. A bus stop is just a few steps away, and public parking is available along the street on a first-come, first-served basis — a convenience for you and your guests alike.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I rent a desk for just one day?

Yes. A desk costs 15€ per day, 50€ per week or 140€ per month — choose the option that suits you best.

Is the desk reserved exclusively for me?

Yes. A dedicated desk gives you a fixed spot, with a Steelcase ergonomic chair and a monitor, that you do not share with anyone.

Can I use the Maccollab address as a registered office?

Yes. We offer registered office services at our address on Strada Horia 22A, Brașov — a professional address for your business. Get in touch and we will prepare a tailored offer.

Can I access the space outside regular hours?

Yes. The space is accessible 24/7, every day of the week, so you can work at your own pace.

Is there parking nearby?

Yes. Public parking is available along Strada Horia on a first-come, first-served basis. If you prefer public transport, a bus stop is just a few steps from the building.

Would you like to see the space?

We would be delighted to show you around, with no obligation. Write to us or call us directly.
