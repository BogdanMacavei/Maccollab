Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in the price?

The price includes the fully furnished office, high-speed internet, air conditioning, cleaning, tea, coffee and water, as well as access to the shared areas. The conference room is available upon booking.

Can I use the Maccollab address as a registered office?

Yes. We offer registered office services at our address on Strada Horia 22A, Brașov — a professional address for your business. Get in touch and we will prepare a tailored offer.

How long is the contract?

We work with a flexible monthly contract, with no long-term commitment, which you can adapt as your needs evolve.

Can I access the space outside regular hours?

Yes. The space is accessible 24/7, every day of the week, so you can work at your own pace.

Is there parking nearby?

Yes. Free public parking is available along Strada Horia on a first-come, first-served basis. If you prefer public transport, a bus stop is just a few steps from the building.

Would you like to see the space?

We would be delighted to show you around, with no obligation. Write to us or call us directly.
