Conference Room for Rent in Brașov

Meetings & presentations

A fully equipped, professional conference room available for booking by everyone — whether you're a Maccollab member or not. Perfect for client meetings, team workshops, presentations, and interviews. The space is designed to impress and built to perform. It can be booked by the hour or for a full day, and awaits you at Strada Horia 22A, Brașov.

Location & Access

You will find us at Strada Horia 22A, Brașov, in an easily accessible location. A bus stop is just a few steps away, and public parking is available along the street on a first-come, first-served basis — a convenience for you and your guests alike.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the room be booked by the hour?

Yes. The conference room can be booked by the hour or for a full day (120€ per day). For hourly rates and availability, feel free to write to us or call us at any time.

Do I need to be a Maccollab member?

No. The room can be booked by anyone — companies, freelancers, trainers or event organisers.

What is included in the price?

The price includes the display screen and projector, high-speed internet, air conditioning, tea, coffee and water for your guests, printer access, and the room prepared before every booking.

How many people does the room hold?

The room comfortably seats up to 10 people around a large meeting table.

Is there parking nearby?

Yes. Public parking is available along Strada Horia on a first-come, first-served basis. If you prefer public transport, a bus stop is just a few steps from the building.

Would you like to see the space?

We would be delighted to show you around, with no obligation. Write to us or call us directly.
