Location

How to Get to Maccollab

You will find us at Strada Horia 22A in Brașov, a 15-minute walk from Piața Sfatului. The building is easy to spot by its black decorative fence on the street. Below you have the address, the map, the bus stops, parking information and photos of the way to the entrance.

  • Address

    Strada Horia 22A, 500045 Brașov, Romania

  • By bus

    Get off at the Memorandului or the Bisericii Române stop, served by lines 5, 14, 24, 28 and A1. From either stop it is about a 5-minute walk to us.

  • On foot

    From Piața Sfatului, the historic centre of Brașov, Maccollab is about a 15-minute walk.

  • By car

    Free public parking is available along Strada Horia on a first-come, first-served basis.

  • Opening hours

    Access 24/7, every day of the week, with a key card. If you would like a tour, please book it in advance so we can welcome you.

  • Contact

    +40 731 624 845 · office@maccollab.com

Finding the entrance

From the street, go through the black gate with the intercom, follow the path along the building and you will reach the wooden door with the “22A maccollab” sign. If you have booked a tour, ring the intercom or call us and we will come and meet you.
